How we play

Our goal is to be the premier men's league team in the South Bay that wins each league we enter. We value and emphasize playing together and playing hard defense. Defense and playing hard will be our calling card.

We value guys who respect the game, play the right way, and bring good energy and camaraderie. We'll move the ball, rebound, set screens, and do the little things that win games. We're not here for flash — we're here to be consistent, compete, and win as a team.
